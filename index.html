Pocket Golf demo

Your phone is the club grip. Hold it in both hands, screen up, and look down at the ball.

  1. Pick a club (top left) and set the power with the slider. The yellow ring shows where it lands.
  2. Look down at the ball and tap Set. You're lined up on the target.
  3. Swing smoothly. Speed doesn't matter, just enough to get through the ball. What matters is the line: don't twist the phone (that turns the face) and don't tip it toward or away from you (that moves the club off the ball's line).
